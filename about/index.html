About Me

My Background

I am Nigel Denaya Manuwu Nayar, a student in the Department of Electrical Engineering at the University of Indonesia (Class of 2023). As a self-taught software engineer and a passionate electrical engineer, I bridge the gap between hardware design and software development.

My Technical Focus

My work is primarily driven by a deep interest in robotics, where I have taken leadership roles such as the Lead of the Electrical Division for the Reignblaze team (TRUI). I have developed a strong technical foundation in:

  • Embedded Systems: Extensive experience with ESP32 and Arduino, implementing features like FreeRTOS and I2C integration.
  • Machine Learning: Development of neural networks and classification models, including SVM, BPNN, CNN, and LSTM.
  • VLSI & Semiconductors: Experience in CMOS layouts and semiconductor physics using tools like Xschem, Ngspice, and Skywater or IHPSG13G2 130nm PDK.
