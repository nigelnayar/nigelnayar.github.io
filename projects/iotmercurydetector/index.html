IoT-Based Mercury Content in Water Detector (NAMHED)

KiHajar STEM Competition 2022 National Finalist Project

Arduino-based simple measurement system for detecting illegal gold mining (PETI) water contamination.

Nigel Adnan Mutia Hydrargyrum Measuring Device : NAMHED

Project Overview

NAMHED [Nigel Adnan Mutia Hydrargyrum Measuring Device] is an innovation created using a semi-automatic system.It functions as a tool to measure the mercury content in a solution by utilizing the mass changes that occur at an inert cathode after electrolysis. Conventional mercury measuring technology is currently known to be relatively expensive and scarce. With the creation of this device, hard-to-reach areas can easily and practically determine mercury levels in water due to illegal mining in rivers near local residents. The NAMHED innovation is expected to inspire the younger generation in the current 4.0 era, with several advantages: it is economical, environmentally friendly, operates quickly and is easy to use, and its small size makes it easy to carry anywhere. This contrasts with conventional devices, which are known to be larger.

Problem Background & Field Findings

According to Indonesian Health Ministry Regulation (Permenkes No. 492/2010), safe drinking water must contain no more than 0.001 mg/L of mercury. Field interviews conducted with riverbank residents in Mendawai revealed that while communities regularly experience iron odor and skin irritation from river exposure, awareness of latent mercury toxicity remains low due to a lack of immediate visual feedback and absent local test kits.

Measurement Principle & Working Mechanism

System Mechanics

The device measures mercury concentrations by combining a localized electrolysis stage with a load-cell mass sensor translated through an Arduino unit:

Step 1: Electrolysis Deposition

A 100 mL water sample undergoes simple electrolysis. Dissolved mercury ions ($\text{Hg}^+$) migrate and precipitate directly onto the cathode surface.

100 mL Sample $\text{Hg}^+$ Deposition
Step 2: Signal Conditioning & Data Acquisition

The dried cathode is placed onto a load-cell platform connected to an HX711 24-bit ADC amplifier module. The HX711 translates micro-gram physical weight shifts into digital signals readable by the Arduino micro-controller.

Load Cell HX711 ADC Module Arduino UNO
Step 3: Interactive Calculation & Display

Using a matrix keypad, the user inputs the initial cathode weight ($m_1$) and sample volume ($V = 100\text{ mL}$). The Arduino computes the mass difference ($\Delta m = m_2 - m_1$) to display the net mercury concentration ($\text{mg/L}$) on an $16\times2$ LCD screen.

4x4 Keypad LCD 16x2 Output

Experimental Results & Analysis

Testing across eight standardized 100 mL samples ($S_0$ through $S_7$) produced key environmental insights:

Key Testing Conclusions

  • Highest Contamination ($S_5$): Collected from the Rungan River near active illegal gold mining zones, exhibiting the maximum recorded mercury concentration.
  • Lowest River Sample ($S_2$): Collected from urban sections of the Kahayan River with high residential activity but distant from active PETI mining sites.
  • Mitigation Recommendations: Local miners should transition from mercury amalgamation to eco-friendly alternatives such as cyanidation leaching, iGoli leaching, or gravity separation technology.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Here are some questions asked about our device. (It's in Indonesian. Make sure you are familiar with the language!)

Q1: Mengapa kalian dapat menyimpulkan bahwa yang menempel di batang katode adalah merkuri?

Kami masih belum bisa 100% menyimpulkan bahwa senyawa yang menempel adalah merkuri. Alat kami masih berupa prototype atau rancangan awal sehingga masih perlu dilakukan pengembangan dan penilitian lebih lanjut

Q2: Apa yang terjadi pada manusia jika terpapar merkuri?

Penelitian oleh Stephan Bose pada tahun 2007 s/d 2016, pada responden menunjukkan gejala spesifik keracunan seperti movement disorder dan gangguan pada saraf. Selain itu, menyebabkan penyakit minamata atau kelainan pada system saraf pusat karena ikan yang terkontaminasi merkuri dan menyebabkan mulai dari kejangkejang, kelumpuhan hingga kematian. Ada pula kerusakan paru-paru, pencernaan, ginjal, kulit, kanker, gangguan pada janin dan bayi, dengan gejala awal kesemutan pada kaki dan tangan, lemas, penyempitan sudut pandang, penurunan kemampuan berbicara dan pendengaran, serta tremor dan kelumpuhan.

Q3: Bagaimana cara menghilangkan merkuri pada air?

Menurut salah satu penelitian yang dilakukan oleh Hidayati pada tahun 2004 bahwa Usaha pengolahan tersebut menggunakan agen biologi yakni Remediasi yang menggunakan tumbuhan atau disebut Teknologi Fitoremediasi. Fitoremediasi didefinisikan sebagai pencucian polutan yang dimediasi oleh tumbuhan, termasuk pohon, rumputrumputan, dan tanaman air. Yang salah satu penerapannya Memanfaatkan pasir zeolite atau dengan melati air [Echinodorus Paleafolius]. Yang dimana pasir zeolite akan menyaring partikel partikel besar pada air dan melati air akan mencuci polutan pada air sungai.
